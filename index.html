Got questions? We've got answers.

What file formats can I convert? ▼
PDF, DOCX, DOC, PPT, PPTX, XLSX, TXT, JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP — over 10 formats supported. Just about any document or image works.
Is my data secure? ▼
Absolutely. We use 256-bit encryption for all transfers. Uploaded files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers after 1 hour.
How long does a conversion take? ▼
Most conversions complete in under 10 seconds. Larger files may take up to 30 seconds. Our optimized engine ensures fast processing.
Is there a file size limit? ▼
The default limit is 50MB per file — enough for most documents and presentations.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No account needed! TransPDF works completely free without registration. Just upload and convert.
What about DOC, PPT, XLS formats? ▼
The newer formats (DOCX, PPTX, XLSX) work directly. For legacy formats (DOC, PPT, XLS), we extract text content. For full conversion, install LibreOffice on your server.
